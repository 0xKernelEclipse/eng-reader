How to Install & Use on iPhone

  1. 1 Open the site in Safari while online and wait until offline files are cached.
  2. 2 Tap the Safari Share button and choose 'Add to Home Screen'.
  3. 3 Close Safari, open the app from your home screen, and use it freely even offline!
